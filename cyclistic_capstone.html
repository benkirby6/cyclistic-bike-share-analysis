Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis: Converting Casual Riders into Annual Members

Data Analytics Capstone

November 2026

1. Ask: Business Task & Executive Summary

Cyclistic’s marketing team wants to maximize the number of annual memberships by converting existing casual riders (single-ride and full-day pass users) into annual subscribers. An analysis of 6.13 million valid rides from November 2025 through October 2026 reveals clear behavioral differences between the two groups:

  • Ridership Share: Annual members account for 64.7% of total rides (3,967,675 trips), while casual riders account for 35.3% (2,161,947 trips).
  • Trip Duration: Casual riders average 18.0 minutes per ride—nearly 50% longer than annual members (12.1 minutes).
  • Usage Patterns: Members ride consistently on weekdays for commuting, whereas casual riders surge on weekends and during warm summer months for leisure.

2. Prepare: Data Sources & Limitations

  • Data Source: Public historical trip data provided by Motivate International Inc. (Divvy bike-share in Chicago) under the official data license agreement, covering the trailing 12 months from November 2025 through October 2026 (202511 to 202610).
  • Data Limitations: Personally identifiable information (PII) such as rider IDs and credit card details is stripped for privacy, preventing tracking of individual repeat casual riders across multiple trips.

3. Process: Data Cleaning & Manipulation

Using R (tidyverse and lubridate), all 12 monthly datasets were standardized and filtered to remove test/maintenance trips under 1 minute, extreme outliers exceeding 24 hours (1,440 minutes), and null classifications—resulting in 6,129,622 valid rides. Derived metrics included ride_length_mins, day_of_week, and month.

4. Analyze: Summary of Analysis

Across the 12-month period, annual members generated 64.7% of total ride volume (3,967,675 trips) while casual riders generated 35.3% (2,161,947 trips). Despite taking fewer trips overall, casual riders averaged 18.0 minutes per ride compared to 12.1 minutes for annual members—nearly 50% longer per trip. Behavioral segmentation by day of week and month confirms two distinct user profiles: annual members rely on the service for consistent weekday commuting, whereas casual riders primarily use bikes for longer leisure trips on weekends and during peak summer weather.

5. Share: Supporting Visualizations & Key Findings

A. Weekly Ridership Volume: Commuters vs. Weekend Leisure

Annual members peak Tuesday through Thursday, reflecting routine work commutes. Casual riders show the exact opposite pattern, peaking sharply on Saturdays and Sundays.

B. Average Ride Duration by Day of Week

Across every single day of the week, casual riders take significantly longer trips than annual members, peaking on weekends.

C. Monthly Seasonality Trends

While annual members maintain steady baseline ridership year-round, casual ridership is highly seasonal—climbing sharply from May through September before dropping in winter.

6. Act: Top 3 Strategic Recommendations

  1. Introduce a Seasonal / Weekend Leisure Pass
    • Target & Tactic: Launch a lower-barrier “Summer Pass” (May–September) or “Weekend Warrior” subscription tier tailored to recreational riders who peak on Saturdays and Sundays.
    • Expected Impact: Captures high-volume summer and weekend casual demand while creating an easy upsell path to full annual membership.
  2. Trigger Automated Post-Ride Cost-Savings Prompts
    • Target & Tactic: Deploy in-app push notifications immediately after a casual rider completes a trip exceeding 15 minutes (below their 18.0-minute average) or takes a second ride within 7 days, displaying exact dollar savings under an annual plan.
    • Expected Impact: Converts high-frequency or long-duration casual riders at the exact moment they experience the highest single-ride pricing friction.
  3. Target Weekday Casual Commuters with a 7-Day Free Trial
    • Target & Tactic: Segment casual riders unlocking bikes during peak weekday commute windows (7–9 AM and 4–6 PM) and offer a frictionless 7-day free trial of annual membership perks.
    • Expected Impact: Directly converts existing weekday commuters into recurring annual subscribers by letting them experience unlimited commuter unlocks risk-free.
